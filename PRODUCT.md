# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- Primary: apprenants (enterprise learners) progressing through certified SAP training paths (SAP, IBN Group, etc.).
- Secondary: formateurs, admins, entreprises / universités (B2B customer context).

## Product Purpose
- Web platform for SAP e-learning: structured learning paths, courses, final projects, assessments, defenses, certifications.
- Goal: validated, certified learning progression end to end.

## Brand Personality
- 3 words: Rigueur, Confiance, Expertise.
- Tone: professional, institutional, calm, authoritative without coldness.

## Anti-references
- Generic pastel SaaS: pastel background, indigo blue, heavily rounded buttons, boring identical cards.

## Design Principles
1. Institutional confidence over decoration: design should read as credible and rigorous, not playful or trendy.
2. Pedagogical clarity: learning progress, hierarchy and comprehension are first-class design concerns.
3. Rigor as a design virtue: tight, precise, disciplined visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target: WCAG AA. Mobile-first, keyboard accessible, reduced-motion support, strong contrast.
